# SBI vs HDFC Bank — Financial Analysis, Forecasting & Valuation

## Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive comparative financial analysis of **State Bank of India (SBI)** and **HDFC Bank**, focusing on financial statement analysis, bank-specific performance metrics, forecasting, three-statement modelling, and equity valuation.

The project evaluates both banks across profitability, liquidity, asset quality, capital adequacy, efficiency, growth, and shareholder returns, followed by forward-looking financial projections and valuation using **FCFF DCF and Dividend Discount Models (DDM)**.

## Key Areas Covered

* Analysed the **Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement** of SBI and HDFC Bank.
* Performed **horizontal analysis** to evaluate year-over-year changes in major financial statement line items.
* Performed **vertical analysis** to understand the composition of each bank's financial statements.
* Built a comprehensive **banking ratio analysis** covering:

  * Profitability
  * Liquidity
  * Growth
  * Asset Quality
  * Capital Adequacy
  * Efficiency
* Analysed key banking metrics including **ROA, ROE, NIM, Net Profit Margin, Cost-to-Income Ratio, Loan-to-Deposit Ratio, Gross NPA, Net NPA, Provision Coverage Ratio, CET1 Ratio, Tier 1 Capital Ratio, CRAR, Leverage Ratio, Loan Growth, Deposit Growth, NII Growth, and Net Profit Growth**.
* Compared SBI and HDFC Bank to identify differences in **profitability, asset quality, capital strength, operating efficiency, liquidity, and growth**.
* Developed separate **three-statement forecasting models** for both banks, linking the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement.
* Forecasted key financial drivers and operating metrics to develop forward-looking financial statements.
* Built **FCFF-based DCF valuation models** for both SBI and HDFC Bank, incorporating projected operating performance, unlevered free cash flow, WACC, terminal value, enterprise value, equity value, and implied share price.
* Built **Dividend Discount Models (DDM)** using projected dividend per share, payout ratios, cost of equity, terminal growth, and terminal value.
* Created a dedicated **valuation comparison** to compare current market prices with FCFF DCF and DDM implied values.
* Evaluated the differences between DCF and DDM results and considered the impact of **dividend payout and retained earnings** on bank valuation.

## Valuation Framework

The valuation analysis uses two approaches:

### FCFF DCF

**Forecast Financial Performance → EBIT → EBIAT → Unlevered FCF → Terminal Value → Enterprise Value → Equity Value → Implied Share Price**

### Dividend Discount Model

**Net Profit → Dividend Payout → Dividend Per Share → Present Value of Dividends → Terminal Value → Intrinsic Value Per Share**

The project also compares the two methodologies to understand why DDM and DCF can produce materially different valuation outcomes for banks with relatively low dividend payout ratios and significant retained earnings.

## Key Insights Evaluated

The analysis focuses on questions such as:

* Which bank generates stronger returns on assets and equity?
* Which bank demonstrates stronger net interest margin and operating efficiency?
* How do SBI and HDFC compare in terms of asset quality and NPA levels?
* Which bank maintains stronger capital adequacy and leverage metrics?
* How do loan and deposit growth differ between the two banks?
* How do the banks' projected financial performance compare?
* What are the implied equity values under DCF and DDM?
* Why can DDM and DCF produce different intrinsic values for banks?
* How sensitive are valuation conclusions to assumptions such as **WACC, cost of equity, and terminal growth**?
